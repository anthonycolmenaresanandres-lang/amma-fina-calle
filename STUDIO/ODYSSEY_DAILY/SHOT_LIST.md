# ODYSSEY DAILY — Shot List & Craft Curriculum (30 days ≈ 5-minute short)

Six movements. One shot per day, 10 seconds each. Each day teaches one craft
topic and the shot is the exercise. Shots are designed to cut in order.
Sequence beats follow Butler's public-domain translation.

## I. The Wine-Dark Sea (departure)

| Day | Shot | Craft topic | Shot description (10s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | s001 | Establishing wide + rule of thirds | Extreme wide, dawn: a lone penteconter tiny on swelling wine-dark sea, horizon on lower third, bone sail catching first gold light. Slow natural drift only. |
| 02 | s002 | Medium shot & blocking | Deck midship: rowers pull in unison, medium shot across the benches, spray over the gunwale. |
| 03 | s003 | Close-up & the face as landscape | Odysseus at the steering oar, close-up, salt wind in beard, eyes fixed past camera. |
| 04 | s004 | Insert / extreme close-up | ECU: weathered hand tightens rough rope around wooden cleat, water beads on bronze ring. |
| 05 | s005 | High angle for geography | High overhead of the ship's wake cutting the sea, oars feathering in rhythm. |

## II. The Cyclops (fear and scale)

| Day | Shot | Craft topic | Shot description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 06 | s006 | Low angle for power | Cave mouth from below: a colossal one-eyed silhouette fills the entrance, sheep at his knees, men like insects at frame bottom. |
| 07 | s007 | Dutch tilt for unease | Tilted frame inside the cave: firelight, men pressed against wet rock, shadows crawling. |
| 08 | s008 | Over-the-shoulder framing | OTS past Odysseus' shoulder: the glowing stake tip approaches the sleeping giant's closed eye. Cut before contact. |
| 09 | s009 | Silhouette & backlight | Men strapped beneath rams, backlit at cave mouth, dawn blazing behind wool. |
| 10 | s010 | Wide-to-reveal (dolly out) | Start close on Odysseus shouting defiance from the stern; pull back to reveal a hurled boulder crashing into the sea beside the ship. |

## III. Winds, Witch, and the Dead

| Day | Shot | Craft topic | Shot description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | s011 | Match cut (shape) | The tied mouth of the ox-hide bag of winds fills frame — designed to cut FROM s010's round boulder splash. Hands loosen the cord; a silver whirl escapes. |
| 12 | s012 | Handheld energy | Storm chaos on deck, handheld feel: rigging whips, men grab lines, rain lashes diagonally. |
| 13 | s013 | Tracking lateral | Track alongside Circe's colonnade: she walks past marble columns, a loom behind her, wolves and lions pacing tame at her hem. |
| 14 | s014 | Slow motion | Wine pours into a golden cup in extreme slow motion, smoke of the drug curling above it, firelight refraction. |
| 15 | s015 | Fog & atmosphere depth | The Underworld: figures emerge in layers from grey mist across a black river, each layer paler than the last. |

## IV. The Sirens' Strait

| Day | Shot | Craft topic | Shot description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | s016 | POV shot | POV from the mast: rope crosses frame edges, the deck sways below, men row with wax-stopped ears, one face straining upward — his. |
| 17 | s017 | Reaction shot | Close on Odysseus bound to the mast, agony and ecstasy fighting in his face, veins against rope. |
| 18 | s018 | Crane rise | Camera rises from deck level up the mast to reveal the strait ahead: cliffs on both sides, white water between. |
| 19 | s019 | Cross-cut pair A (danger left) | Scylla's cliff: six serpentine necks uncoil from a black cave in the rock face above the ship. |
| 20 | s020 | Cross-cut pair B (danger right) | Charybdis: the sea itself opens — a rotating glass-walled funnel of water, debris orbiting the throat. |

## V. Calypso and the Raft (longing)

| Day | Shot | Craft topic | Shot description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | s021 | Golden hour vs blue hour | Odysseus alone on Calypso's beach at blue hour, sitting where surf reaches his feet, staring at a dark horizon. |
| 22 | s022 | Foreground framing (depth) | Through hanging vines and cave flowers: Calypso watches him from her fire-lit grotto, framed in layers. |
| 23 | s023 | Match on action | His axe strikes a pine — cut on impact to the log dropping onto the growing raft. One continuous motion across the cut. |
| 24 | s024 | Practical light (fire) | Night: he steers the raft by starlight, face lit only by a small clay fire-pot, Pleiades sharp above. |
| 25 | s025 | The storm (movement + scale) | Poseidon's storm shatters the raft: a mountain of black water, the tiny figure clinging to a beam. |

## VI. Ithaca (return)

| Day | Shot | Craft topic | Shot description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | s026 | Reflections | Dawn calm: the shoreline of Ithaca doubled in still water, one swimmer's ripple breaking the mirror. |
| 27 | s027 | Whip pan | In the hall: whip pan from sneering suitors' table to the ragged beggar at the threshold. |
| 28 | s028 | The long-take feel | One unbroken slow push down the hall's central aisle: braziers, suitors falling silent one by one, the bow waiting on its peg. |
| 29 | s029 | Insert pays off (callback to s004) | ECU: the same weathered hands — now stringing the great bow. The cord seats. Held breath. |
| 30 | s030 | Climax: wide → close pattern | The arrow flies through twelve axe-heads in one line; end close on Odysseus' face, finally home. Hold to black. |

## Prompt seeds (shared prefix, keep verbatim)

> Bronze Age Aegean, ~1200 BC. Cinematic film still look, 24fps, natural light
> only, wine-dark teal sea, bronze and oxblood palette, weathered textures,
> epic scale, no text, no watermark, no modern objects.
