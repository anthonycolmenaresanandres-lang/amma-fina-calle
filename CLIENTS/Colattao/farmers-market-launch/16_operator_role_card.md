# Operator Role Card — Market Manager

One page. This is everything the Market Manager needs to run the Colattao
Community Market every month without Anthony. Added 2026-06-18. Pairs with
`15_monthly_operating_system.md`.

## Your role in one line

Run the same monthly loop, keep vendors and the lot organized, and only escalate
to Anthony the few things that are his call.

## Time & pay

About **3–4 hours per month**, plus the market morning. **Paid position: ~$200
per market** (confirm exact rate when hired). You also coordinate the **parking /
entry guide** (~$100/market, or an off-duty police officer if required).

## Your monthly checklist (same every month)

**Week 1 — Open**
- [ ] Confirm the date is the fixed slot (2nd Saturday) and rain date (3rd Saturday).
- [ ] OS opens vendor applications; send the standing roster their "you're in" note.

**Week 2 — Approve**
- [ ] Review applications. Approve vendors that fit the allowed categories
      (arts, crafts, makers, flowers, gifts, packaged goods).
- [ ] Anything new/unusual (a new category, prepared food, a sponsor) → send to
      Anthony for yes/no. Do NOT approve those yourself.
- [ ] Confirm the vendor count.

**Week 3 — Remind + collect**
- [ ] OS sends reminders + the online pre-pay link.
- [ ] Confirm vendors have paid online (no cash). Unpaid by Thursday = off the list.
- [ ] QR vendor directory updates automatically as vendors pay.

**Week 4 — Prep**
- [ ] Print the site map and the check-in sheet.
- [ ] Confirm the day-of helper **and the parking/entry guide** (or off-duty officer if used).
- [ ] Send vendors the final load-in time + their booth number.

**Event morning**
- [ ] 7:30 — vendor load-in begins; check vendors in via the OS.
- [ ] 8:30 — **parking/entry guide in position outside** to direct parking and walk customers into the bazaar zone.
- [ ] 8:45 — info/QR hub ready at the zone entrance.
- [ ] 9:00 — open. 1:00 — close.
- [ ] After close — helper sweeps the lot; you confirm the lot is clear by 2:00.
- [ ] OS logs attendance; sponsor report goes out automatically.

## When to escalate to Anthony (and ONLY these)

- A new vendor **category** not on the approved list.
- Any **prepared/cooked food** request (must be approved tenant or approved food truck).
- A **sponsor** deal.
- Anything that affects the **landlord relationship, insurance, or a city rule**.
- A safety/emergency issue.

Everything else: you decide and run it.

## Tools

- Fina Calle OS market module — applications, approvals, reminders, QR directory,
  check-in, reporting.
- This card + `15_monthly_operating_system.md` for the full picture.
- `06_vendor_layout_plan.md` + `13_site_map.md` for booth placement.

## The standing rules (never break these)

- No public date until landlord + city/zoning + fire + health are confirmed.
- Booth fees are **online pre-pay only** — never handle cash.
- Keep fire lanes, drive aisles, ramps, sidewalks, and accessible parking clear.
- Cooked food = approved tenants or approved food trucks only.
- Market money stays in its own ledger, separate from the Fina Calle OS fee.
