# Colattao Community Market — One-Page Summary

**What it is:** a small **monthly** arts-and-crafts bazaar in the yellow
parking-space patch at Colattao (1115 Independence Blvd, Virginia Beach).
Pilot = **2nd Saturday, 9 AM–1 PM, 8–12 vendors**. Run by a **hired Market
Manager** so it does not depend on Anthony.

**THE ONE RULE:** do not announce a public date until **4 approvals** are in —
**landlord · city/zoning · fire · health.**

## Steps, in order
1. **Landlord first** — send the approval packet (`00`) + permission letter (`02`).
2. **Site walk** — count parking, mark entrances, drive aisles, the yellow closure zone, fire lanes, trash/restroom access (`04`).
3. **Neighbor tenants** — get support / no-objection (`03`).
4. **City calls** — Special Events, Zoning, Fire, Health (scripts in `05`).
5. **Insurance** — get an event-liability quote (the landlord will require it).
6. **Final landlord sign-off** — on the site map, date, vendor count, and rules.
7. **THEN go public** — announce the date, recruit vendors, sell ≥1 sponsor.
8. **Run month 1**, then repeat the same loop every 2nd Saturday.

**Do this next:** send `00` + `02` to the property manager. Nothing else moves until they say yes.

## Winning factors (what makes it work)
- Approvals secured **in order** before any public date.
- Parking, fire lanes, ADA, and tenant doors all stay clear.
- Neighbor tenants support it (or at least don't object).
- City sees it framed correctly as a **small private-property pilot**.
- Cooked food kept **off the tables** (inside tenants / approved food trucks only).
- Strong vendor mix: arts, crafts, makers, flowers, gifts, packaged goods.
- **At least one sponsor per market** (covers the staff).
- Vendors **pre-pay online** (no cash to manage).
- A **hired Market Manager** runs it — not Anthony.

## Losing factors (what kills it)
- Announcing a date **before** approvals.
- Blocking drive aisles, fire lanes, accessible parking, or tenant entrances.
- Cooked food on tables with **no health approval**.
- Competing with Nara Sushi, Subway, or Colattao without their OK.
- **No event insurance.**
- A vague or wrong description to the city.
- Cash handling, or an unclear parking/walking path.
- The whole thing **depending on Anthony** (single point of failure).
- **No sponsor** — staff costs eat the profit.

**Money in one line:** vendor booth fees + one sponsor cover staff and supplies;
the market keeps its **own money, separate** from the $900 + $150/mo Fina Calle OS
fee. Detail: `08` (money), `15` (monthly system), `07` (full checklists).
