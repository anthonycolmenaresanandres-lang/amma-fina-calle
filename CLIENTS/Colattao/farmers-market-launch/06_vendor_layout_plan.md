# Vendor Layout Plan

Site: 1115 Independence Blvd Suite 101/102, Virginia Beach, VA 23455  
Market: Colattao Community Market  
Rule: vendor placement is conceptual until confirmed by property manager, zoning/city, fire, accessibility, traffic, and health path.

## Layout Principles

- Yellow parking-space patch is the proposed closed vendor-table bazaar zone.
- Vendors go in that closed yellow zone first, with overflow only on approved parking edges or other approved areas.
- No booths in drive aisles.
- No booths in fire lanes.
- No booths in accessible parking spaces or access aisles.
- No blocked tenant entrances.
- No blocked ramps or sidewalks.
- Cooked/prepared food stays inside approved tenants or separately approved food trucks only.
- Any food truck or prepared-food vendor needs extra landlord/city/fire/health review.
- Vendor loading happens before market opens.

## Pilot Layout: 8 Vendors

Best use: first approval-friendly pilot.

Suggested arrangement:

- Yellow parking-space patch: 8 vendor tables plus Colattao/check-in/QR at the entrance if spacing allows.
- Edge A/B: no vendor overflow unless yellow patch cannot safely fit 8.
- Customer parking: preserved in main central spaces.
- Vendor parking/loading: assigned to a far approved edge after unloading.
- Food: cooked/prepared food inside approved tenants or separately approved food trucks only.

Operational fit:

- Easy for landlord to evaluate.
- Lower parking impact.
- Lower fire/city complexity.
- Strong first test for tenant feedback.

## Pilot Layout: 12 Vendors

Best use: first event if site walk proves enough edge space and tenant support.

Suggested arrangement:

- Yellow parking-space patch: 10-12 vendor tables if the closure area safely fits them.
- Edge A/B/C: overflow only if property/fire/city reviewers prefer spreading tables out.
- Customer parking: preserve central and front parking where possible.
- Vendor loading: one-way load-in route if site allows.
- Food: cooked/prepared food inside approved tenants or separately approved food trucks only.

Operational fit:

- More revenue than 8 vendors.
- Still small enough for a controlled Saturday pilot.
- Requires stronger parking and tenant support proof.

## Future Expansion: 20 Vendors

Only consider after the pilot succeeds and after written or logged support from property manager, zoning/city, fire, health path, and tenants.

Suggested arrangement:

- Yellow parking-space patch: core bazaar row and market info.
- Edge A: overflow vendor row if approved.
- Edge B: overflow vendor row if approved.
- Edge C/D: future expansion only after pilot data proves parking/traffic works.
- Customer parking: must be measured and protected.
- Vendor parking: separate approved area.
- Traffic controls: may require stronger signage, cones, staff, or police/city review.

Expansion trigger:

- No tenant objections after pilot.
- No parking complaints.
- No blocked access.
- No trash or restroom issues.
- Clear fire/health/city approval path.
- Vendor demand exceeds pilot capacity.

## Category Placement Strategy

- Put crafts, makers, flowers, plants, gifts, packaged goods, and local art inside the yellow bazaar zone.
- Put food trucks only where landlord/city/fire/health review allows safe setup.
- Keep any line-forming vendors away from tenant doors and drive aisles.
- Keep Colattao/check-in/QR table at the yellow zone entrance to prevent confusion.
- Avoid vendors that directly compete with Nara Sushi, Subway, or Colattao unless those tenants approve.

## Vendor Loading And Parking

Recommended pilot schedule:

- 7:30 AM to 8:30 AM: vendor load-in.
- 8:30 AM to 8:45 AM: vendor vehicles move to approved vendor parking.
- 8:45 AM: walking path and drive aisles checked.
- 9:00 AM: market opens.
- 1:00 PM: market closes.
- 1:15 PM to 2:00 PM: vendor load-out.
- 2:00 PM: site sweep complete.

Rules:

- No vendor vehicle remains in booth area unless specifically approved.
- No double parking in drive aisles.
- No loading/unloading at tenant entrances.
- Food vendors unload first only if their inspection/setup path requires it.
