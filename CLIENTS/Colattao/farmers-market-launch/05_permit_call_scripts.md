## Call 1: Virginia Beach Outdoor Event / Special Events

Primary question: Does a small private-property farmers/community market pilot require an outdoor event permit or other event review?

Script:

Hello, my name is Anthony. I am helping Colattao Coffee House at 1115 Independence Blvd Suite 101/102 explore a small community market pilot called Colattao Community Market.

The proposed pilot is 8-12 arts-and-crafts bazaar vendors on a Saturday from 9 AM to 1 PM. It would be on private shopping-center property, subject to landlord approval. The highlighted yellow parking-space area near Colattao would be temporarily closed as the vendor table zone if approved, with drive aisles, fire lanes, tenant entrances, accessible parking, ramps, sidewalks, and emergency access kept clear. Cooked/prepared food would stay inside approved tenants or in separately approved food trucks only.

Before we announce a public date, I need to confirm whether this requires an outdoor event permit, special event application, site map review, police/traffic review, or any other city approval.

Questions:

- Does this private-property pilot require an outdoor event permit?
- Does the city need a site map before answering?
- Is there a vendor-count threshold or attendance threshold?
- Does the fact that vendors sell arts/crafts/packaged goods from a closed parking-space area change the permit path?
- Does temporarily closing a small group of parking spaces require a traffic, police, fire, or site-plan review?
- Is Planning/Zoning the first review for private property?
- Should I submit through Eproval or contact a specific office?
- Are there insurance, signage, noise, restroom, waste, or traffic requirements?
- Is there anything that would prevent us from holding a small pilot if landlord/fire/health paths are satisfied?

Record:

- Contact name
- Department
- Permit required / not required / needs review
- Application link or email
- Required attachments
- Timeline
- Follow-up owner

## Call 2: Zoning

Primary question: Does the shopping-center zoning allow a temporary outdoor community market/vendor event on private property?

Script:

Hello, my name is Anthony. I am helping Colattao Coffee House at 1115 Independence Blvd Suite 101/102 evaluate a small Saturday community market pilot on private property.

We are not announcing a date yet. We want to confirm the zoning path first. The pilot would be 8-12 arts-and-crafts bazaar vendors, 9 AM to 1 PM. The proposed layout would temporarily close the highlighted yellow parking-space patch for vendor tables if approved, while keeping drive aisles, fire lanes, tenant entrances, accessible spaces, ramps, sidewalks, and emergency access clear. Cooked/prepared food would be limited to approved inside tenants or separately approved food trucks.

Questions:

- Is a temporary outdoor market/vendor event allowed at this property with landlord permission?
- Is a conditional use, temporary use approval, peddler permit, or other zoning approval required?
- Are there limits on vendor count, event frequency, signs, tents, canopies, closed parking spaces, or duration?
- Does each vendor need separate zoning/peddler review, or can the market operate under an event/community license path?
- What should be included on the site map?
- Should food vendors be handled separately with Health and Fire?

Record:

- Zoning contact
- Approval status
- Required application or confirmation
- Restrictions
- Follow-up needed

## Call 3: Fire Prevention

Primary question: What does Fire Prevention need to review for a small outdoor vendor market?

Script:

Hello, my name is Anthony. I am helping Colattao Coffee House at 1115 Independence Blvd Suite 101/102 explore a small community market pilot.

The pilot would be 8-12 arts-and-crafts bazaar vendors on a Saturday from 9 AM to 1 PM. We are planning to temporarily close the highlighted yellow parking-space area near Colattao for tables if approved, while keeping all drive aisles, fire lanes, tenant entrances, emergency access, accessible parking, ramps, and sidewalks clear. Cooked/prepared food would stay inside approved tenants or in separately approved food trucks only. We need to know what Fire Prevention requires before any public announcement.

Questions:

- Do you need to review the site map before the event?
- What clearance do you require for fire lanes and emergency access?
- Are tents/canopies allowed, and are weights/anchoring requirements specified?
- What rules apply to generators, extension cords, temporary electrical, propane, cooking, heating, or open flame?
- Do food trucks or any outside prepared-food vendors require separate fire inspection or permits?
- Are cones/barricades required or prohibited for closing the yellow parking-space patch?
- Are there fire extinguisher requirements for vendors?
- Is a fire inspection fee or on-site inspection required?
- What should be submitted and how far in advance?

Record:

- Fire Prevention contact
- Required clearances
- Allowed/prohibited activities
- Inspection/fee
- Site map notes
- Food/cooking notes

## Call 4: Virginia Beach Health Department Environmental Health

Primary question: What must food/drink vendors do for a temporary market at this site?

Script:

Hello, my name is Anthony. I am helping Colattao Coffee House at 1115 Independence Blvd Suite 101/102 explore a small community market pilot.

The first event would be 8-12 bazaar vendors on a Saturday from 9 AM to 1 PM. The yellow parking-space patch would focus on arts, crafts, flowers, makers, gifts, and packaged/low-risk goods. Cooked/prepared food would not be sold from ordinary yellow-patch table vendors; customers could buy cooked food inside approved tenants, and any food truck/outside prepared-food vendor would be separately approved. We are not announcing a public date until we understand the health department path.

Questions:

- Which vendors need a Temporary Food Establishment permit if the bazaar table zone focuses on arts/crafts and packaged/low-risk goods?
- Are packaged foods, baked goods, produce, coffee/drinks, samples, food trucks, or prepared foods treated differently?
- Does the market organizer need to submit anything, or does each food vendor apply individually?
- How far in advance should applications be submitted?
- Are handwashing stations required for sampling or food handling?
- Are restrooms required for a four-hour pilot?
- Are there rules for food trucks, tents, ice, water, temperature control, or commissaries?
- Are there exemptions that apply to farm/produce vendors?
- Who should vendors contact for the Virginia Beach application?

Record:

- Environmental Health contact
- Vendor categories requiring permits
- Application timeline
- Fees if provided
- Restroom/handwashing guidance
- Inspection requirements
