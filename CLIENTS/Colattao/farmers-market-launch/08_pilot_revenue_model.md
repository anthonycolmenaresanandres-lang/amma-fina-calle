## How Fina Calle OS Justifies $900 Setup + $140/Month

The market does not justify the software only through booth fees. It justifies the software by reducing coordination labor and making approvals, vendor ops, and reporting reusable.

Setup fee value:

- Builds the vendor application intake.
- Creates the approval workflow and evidence log.
- Creates the QR vendor directory.
- Creates market-day check-in.
- Creates permit/status checklist.
- Sets up reminder templates.
- Sets up sponsor and vendor reporting.
- Produces reusable launch assets for repeat markets.

Monthly value:

- Vendor application management.
- Approval status tracking.
- Reminder emails/texts.
- QR directory updates.
- Check-in report.
- Vendor attendance records.
- Sponsor visibility report.
- Tenant/landlord confidence through organized records.
- Repeat-event readiness instead of rebuilding each market manually.

Simple business case:

- At 12 vendors and one sponsor, the market can gross about $790.
- If it runs monthly at standard pricing, 12 vendors at $50 plus one $250 sponsor equals $850 gross before premium vendors.
- Fina Calle OS at $140/month is covered by fewer than 3 standard vendor booths, or one sponsor plus one booth.
- The $900 setup is justified if it helps secure approvals, launch the first event, and creates a repeatable operating system for future events.

## Pricing Notes

- Keep the first pilot friendly at $35 to reduce vendor friction.
- Move to $50 standard only after the pilot proves operations.
- Charge $75 for premium bazaar placement or separately approved food trucks only when the location, health/fire requirements, and customer draw support it.
- Sponsors should receive QR directory placement, banner placement at the yellow bazaar zone if approved, and post-event report.

## Bazaar And Coffee Expo Upside

The pilot should be priced like a friendly arts-and-crafts bazaar, not a heavy food festival. The larger upside is proving the operating model, tenant cooperation, vendor workflow, QR directory, check-in, and reporting. Once that works, Anthony can package the concept into a larger coffee fest / coffee expo that invites regional coffee shops and eventually moves to a convention-center venue.
