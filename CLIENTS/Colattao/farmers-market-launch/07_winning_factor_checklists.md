# Winning Factor Checklists

Use this as the approval readiness scorecard. If any approval group is weak, do not announce the event date.

## Landlord / Property Manager

- One-page approval packet is clear and low-risk.
- Yellow parking-space patch is clearly reviewed as a temporary closed vendor-table bazaar zone.
- Vendor areas are limited to approved edges.
- Parking count is documented.
- Parking-space closure count is documented.
- Tenant entrances are protected.
- Fire/emergency access is protected.
- ADA spaces, ramps, and sidewalks are protected.
- Trash plan does not rely on tenant dumpsters without approval.
- Insurance requirements are identified.
- Final site map is approved before public announcement.

## Neighboring Tenants

- Each tenant is contacted before public announcement.
- Script explains this is a small pilot, not a takeover.
- Direct competition is avoided unless approved.
- Nara Sushi and Subway are protected from direct meal competitors.
- Colattao is protected from outside coffee/espresso competitors unless approved; regional coffee-shop participation belongs to the larger future coffee fest/expo concept.
- Recruiting offices maintain walk-in access and visibility.
- Personal-service tenants maintain appointment parking and entrance access.
- Objections are logged and addressed.
- No-objection or support notes are captured in the evidence log.

## Zoning / City

- Private-property use is described accurately.
- Vendor count and hours are provided.
- Site map is available.
- City confirms whether outdoor event permit, zoning review, peddler permit, or other approval applies.
- Signage/noise/parking/event-frequency limits are confirmed.
- Police/traffic review need is confirmed.
- Community Event license path is checked if vendors sell goods.

## Fire

- Site map identifies fire lanes, drive aisles, closed parking spaces, tent/canopy locations, food trucks/prepared-food locations if any, generator/fuel/cooking use, and emergency access.
- Vendor layout does not block emergency access.
- Canopy weights/anchors are addressed.
- Extension cords/generators/propane/open flame are disclosed.
- Food vendors know fire inspection requirements.
- Fire extinguisher requirements are confirmed.
- Inspection/fee/timeline is logged.

## Health Department

- Food vendor categories are separated: no cooked/prepared-food table vendors in the yellow patch; inside tenants and separately approved food trucks are the cooked-food path.
- Temporary Food Establishment path is confirmed.
- Vendor application timeline is confirmed.
- Handwashing, restroom, water, ice, temperature control, and commissary needs are confirmed.
- Food vendors understand they may need individual permits.
- Non-food vendors are clearly separated from food rules.

## Vendors

- Vendor application captures category, products, tent size, power, food status, documents, insurance, and contact info.
- Vendor mix prioritizes arts, crafts, makers, flowers, plants, gifts, and packaged/low-conflict goods.
- Vendor rules protect tenants and access.
- Vendors agree to load-in/load-out windows.
- Vendors pack out trash.
- Vendors know the event can be postponed for approval or weather.
- Vendors are not promised a public date until approvals are confirmed.
- QR vendor directory data is complete before event week.

## Public Customers

- Event is announced only after approvals.
- Parking remains understandable.
- QR directory has printed backup.
- Walking path is clear.
- Vendor mix is family-friendly and useful.
- Restroom guidance is clear if approved.
- Trash is visible and managed.
- Rain/cancellation policy is visible.
