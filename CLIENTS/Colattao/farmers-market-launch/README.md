## Site-Specific Strategy

The highlighted yellow parking-space patch near Colattao should be treated as the proposed closed-off vendor table bazaar zone, not merely a check-in hub. It can hold Colattao presence, vendor check-in, QR vendor directory, sponsor banner, market information, and the first 8-12 arts/crafts/maker vendor tables if the landlord, city/zoning, fire, traffic, and accessibility path confirms the area can be safely closed.

The first pilot should stay small: 8-12 bazaar vendors, no blocked tenant entrances, no blocked drive aisles, no blocked fire lanes, no blocked sidewalks or ramps, no accessible parking used for booths, and clear customer parking. Cooked/prepared food should be handled only by approved inside tenants or separately approved food trucks; customers who want cooked food can go inside.

Long-term vision: prove the small bazaar model first, then use the operating playbook to build toward a larger coffee fest / coffee expo with regional coffee shops, potentially at a convention-center venue.
