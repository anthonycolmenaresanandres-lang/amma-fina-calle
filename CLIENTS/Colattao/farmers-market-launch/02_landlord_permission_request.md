# Landlord / Property Manager Permission Request

Subject: Permission request to explore small Colattao Community Market pilot

Hello [Property Manager Name],

My name is Anthony, and I am helping Colattao Coffee House explore a small neighborhood bazaar concept called the Colattao Community Market at 1115 Independence Blvd Suite 101/102 in Virginia Beach.

We are not asking to announce or launch the event yet. First, we would like permission to explore whether a small, controlled pilot could work on the property.

## Proposed Pilot

- Event: Colattao Community Market
- First format: small pilot
- Vendor count: 8-12 vendors
- Day/time: Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- Public date: not announced until property manager, city/zoning, fire, and health path are confirmed

## Site Concept

The highlighted yellow parking-space patch near Colattao would be reviewed as the closed vendor-table bazaar zone. That area would include:

- Colattao table
- Vendor check-in
- QR vendor directory
- Sponsor banner
- Market information
- 8-12 arts/crafts/maker vendor tables if the space can be safely closed
- Clear customer walking path

The event would focus on arts, crafts, flowers, makers, packaged goods, gifts, and family-friendly vendors. Cooked/prepared food would not be sold from ordinary yellow-patch vendor tables. Customers who want cooked food can come inside to approved tenants, and any food truck or outside prepared-food vendor would require separate approval from the landlord, city/zoning, fire, and health department.

## Property Protections

We will design the pilot around these requirements:

- Keep drive aisles open.
- Keep fire lanes and emergency access open.
- Keep tenant entrances open.
- Do not use accessible parking for booths.
- Do not block ramps, sidewalks, or walking paths.
- Maintain customer parking.
- Avoid direct competition with existing tenants unless approved.
- Keep cooked/prepared food sales inside approved tenants or separately approved food trucks only.
- Keep trash contained and remove market trash after the event.
- Confirm insurance requirements before approval.

## Tenant Outreach

We plan to seek support or no-objection feedback from:

US Air Force Recruiting, Army Recruiting, Nara Sushi, Hair By the C, Dave's Toys and Collectibles, Paris Nails Spa, Subway, Fox Music, and Register Link.
