# Colattao Community Market — Easy Version (Plain Language)

This explains the whole plan in simple words. Anyone can read it.

## What is it?
We want to hold a small market outside Colattao Coffee House.
People set up tables and sell crafts, art, flowers, candles, soaps, and gifts.
It is like a tiny outdoor shopping day next to the coffee shop.

## When is it?
The **second Saturday of every month**. From **9 in the morning to 1 in the afternoon**.
If it rains, we move it to the next Saturday.

## Where is it?
In the marked **yellow parking spaces** next to Colattao.
We block off that small area and put the tables there. People walk around and shop.

## The most important rule
**Do NOT tell the public a date yet.**
First we need **4 "yes" answers**:
1. The **landlord** says yes.
2. The **city** says yes.
3. The **fire department** says yes.
4. The **health department** says yes.

Only after all 4 say yes do we pick a date and tell people.

## The steps, in order
1. Ask the **landlord** first. Give them our letter.
2. **Walk the parking lot.** Count the spaces. Mark where tables go and where cars drive.
3. Talk to the **other stores** next door. Make sure they are okay with it.
4. **Call** the city, fire, and health offices. Ask what we need.
5. Get **insurance**. This protects us if someone gets hurt.
6. Get the landlord's **final yes** on the map and the date.
7. Now **tell people**, invite sellers, and find **one sponsor**.
8. Hold the **first market**. Then do the same thing again next month.

## Who does what
- A **hired helper** (the Market Manager) runs the market. We pay them about **$200** each market.
- **One person** stands outside to help with parking and show people where to go.
  We pay them about **$100**. (Or a **police officer** if the city asks for one.)
- **Anthony** only says **yes or no** to big things. He does not run it.

## The money, made simple
- Sellers pay a small fee to get a table.
- That money pays the helpers and the supplies.
- **One sponsor** (a business that pays to put their name there) helps cover the cost.
- Sellers **pay online before the day**. No cash to carry around.
- Keep this market money in its **own pocket**. Do not mix it with the app/software money.

## What makes it work
- Get the **4 "yes" answers** first.
- Keep the **fire lanes and parking clear**.
- Keep the **neighbors happy**.
- **Sell one sponsor** each time.
- Let the **hired helper** run it.

## What breaks it
- Telling people a date **too early**.
- Blocking cars, fire trucks, or store doors.
- Selling **hot cooked food** on the tables (not allowed unless approved).
- **No insurance**.
- **Anthony trying to do it all** himself.

## The very next thing to do
**Send the landlord the letter and ask for permission.**
Nothing else happens until they say yes.
