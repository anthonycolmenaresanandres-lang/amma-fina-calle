## Mark Yellow Bazaar Zone

Confirm:

- Colattao table location
- Vendor check-in
- QR vendor directory sign
- Sponsor banner location
- Market info sign
- Vendor table rows
- Walking path around and through the bazaar zone

## Mark Trash And Restroom Access

Trash:

- Approved trash/bin locations
- Vendor trash pack-out route
- Final sweep route
- Confirm whether tenant dumpsters may be used. Default: no.

Restrooms:

- Confirm whether Colattao restroom access is available.
- If not, ask health/city whether portable restroom support is needed.
- Mark customer path to restroom if approved.

## Mark Emergency Lane / Fire Access

Before finalizing layout:

- Identify any posted fire lane.
- Mark emergency access route.
- Confirm no tents, tables, signs, banners, lines, or vendor vehicles block access.
- Photograph fire lane/access route.
- Submit site map to Fire Prevention if required.

## ADA / Accessibility

- Accessible parking spaces untouched.
- Access aisles untouched.
- Ramps clear.
- Sidewalks clear.
- Doorways clear.
- Main walking path clear.
- QR directory has printed backup.
