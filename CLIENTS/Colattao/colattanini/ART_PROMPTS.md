## Shared style (use for every character — keep the set consistent)
> Clean modern vector mascot illustration, soft cel shading, bold readable silhouette, die-cut sticker
> style with a thin clean outline, full body, friendly but dynamic soccer pose, transparent background,
> high resolution. Consistent line weight, proportions, and finish across all three so they read as one
> collection. Warm, appetizing colors on the character with subtle gold accents to match the Colattao
> brand (gold #d8b36d). Non-human mascot — simple expressive dot eyes, little stick limbs. No human
> faces, no real people, no club logos, no text, no background.

## 1 — "Churro Latte"  (optional nickname: *Chispa*)
> [shared style] Character 1 of 3: a latte-cup mascot. A warm ceramic coffee cup as the body, filled
> with creamy cinnamon latte and foam dusted with cinnamon; a churro used like a striker's kicking leg.
> Caramel, cinnamon-brown, and cream tones. Mid-kick striker pose, confident and lively.

## 2 — "Coco Beach"  (optional nickname: *Blitz*)
> [shared style] Character 2 of 3: a tropical iced coconut-drink mascot. A clear cup with toasted
> coconut, tropical nuts, and cold foam; a half-coconut shell as a crest/head, a tiny straw. Beachy
> blue, cream, and toasted-coconut tones. Relaxed, flashy finisher pose (a side volley).

## 3 — "California Sandwich"  (optional nickname: *Capitán*)
> [shared style] Character 3 of 3: a croissant-sandwich mascot, the team captain / goalkeeper. A golden
> flaky croissant filled with egg, melty Monterey Jack and queso fresco, and avocado, with bright
> green California-sauce accents and an avocado-green captain's sash. Composed keeper pose, shielding or
> catching the ball.
