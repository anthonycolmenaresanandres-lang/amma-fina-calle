# Fina Calle Sales Demo Script

Source: `CORE_OFFER_199.md` and `FEATURE_STATUS_TABLE.md`.

## Ask first

“What matters most right now: easier menu updates, a more useful in-store scan, or a simple setup?”

Use only the priority the owner states. If none is stated, show the proof without inventing one.

## Thirty-second pitch

### English

“One QR opens your mobile menu and an approved playable experience. You receive private owner access; we handle hosting and link support. It starts at **$199/month per location**. Setup and custom work are separate. I can show you the live Colattao menu and a clearly labeled game-engine demo now.”

### Español

“Un QR abre su menú móvil y una experiencia jugable aprobada. Usted recibe acceso privado; nosotros manejamos el hosting y el soporte del enlace. Empieza en **$199 al mes por local**. La instalación y el trabajo personalizado van por separado. Puedo mostrarle el menú en vivo de Colattao y una demo identificada del motor de juego.”

## Live demo — this order

1. `finacalleos.com/m/colattao` — real client's live menu.
2. `finacalleos.com/penalty-shootout` — playable engine demo, not the buyer's finished game.
3. `finacalleos.com/owner/colattao` — show sign-in only; never fake access.

Never show `/customers`.

## Plain answers

- **What is it?** “A mobile menu, one approved existing game module, and private owner access behind one QR destination.”
- **Download?** “No. The live menu and game demo open in the browser.”
- **Price?** “Starting at $199/month per location. Setup and custom work are quoted separately.”
- **Ordering or payments?** “No. Ordering, payments, checkout, POS, and table service are not in the base plan.”
- **Custom game?** “The demo proves the engine. Custom art or mechanics require a separate written scope.”

## Close

### English

“Would you like the $199/month plan in writing, or would you prefer to review the demo later?”

### Español

“¿Quiere recibir por escrito el plan de $199 al mes, o prefiere revisar la demo más adelante?”
