# Restaurant-owner evidence model

## Known from AMMA materials

- Core beachhead: independent restaurants and cafes in Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads.
- Common current setup: a menu plus social media; the in-store QR experience is often basic.
- Recurring stated objections in the sales package: already has a menu, uses Instagram, does not need a website, is not technical, lacks time, asks about price, says later, or had a bad website-vendor experience.
- Verified proof available: Colattao's public menu, the Fina Calle platform, playable game-engine demos, and the owner sign-in surface.
- Verified offer qualities: mobile browser access, no required customer download, private owner access, and AMMA-handled technical setup/support.
- Current sales intent signals defined by the field checklist: asking about price or timeline, playing the game, sharing a menu or contact route, and agreeing to a pilot conversation.

These are operating inputs, not proven causal patterns or close predictors.

## Working hypotheses to validate

- **Time and complexity:** owners may value low management burden and fast updates.
- **Control:** owners may value the ability to change menu information without waiting on a vendor.
- **Trust:** a working local example and honest limitations may reduce vendor skepticism.
- **Distinctiveness:** a branded, tailored example may create stronger self-recognition than a generic feature list.
- **Risk:** a bounded pilot with written scope may be easier to evaluate than an open-ended technology project.
- **Commercial relevance:** engagement language may work only when connected to an owner-visible metric or business workflow.

Treat each item as `Inferred` until call notes, observed behavior, or conversion data supports it.

## Missing evidence

- Real prospect counts by stage and source.
- Hook, proof, CTA, objection, follow-up, and close rates.
- Verbatim customer or lost-deal language.
- Customer-confirmed workflow costs, retention lift, repeat-visit lift, or revenue impact.
- Segment differences by business type, owner role, language preference, and current technology.
- A verified Colattao testimonial or quantified case-study result.

## Safe observed fields

Record only what was directly stated or observed:

- business type and entry channel;
- stated priority: time, control, brand, engagement, trust, price/risk, or other;
- hook, proof, CTA, and offer shown;
- whether the demo was shown or played;
- objection stated in the prospect's own words;
- response: ignored, curious, asked question, shared material, scheduled, proposed, closed, or declined;
- dated next action and final outcome reason.

Keep real names, emails, phone numbers, and private notes in an owner-controlled private store. Never infer or record protected traits, health, financial distress, intelligence, susceptibility, or personality type.
