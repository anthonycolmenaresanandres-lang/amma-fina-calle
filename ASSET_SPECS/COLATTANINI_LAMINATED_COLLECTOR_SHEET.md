# Colattanini Laminated Collector Sheet

## Purpose

A one-page countertop sheet that explains the Colattanini campaign, shows the three collectible slots, and gives customers a QR path into Penalty Shootout.

## Title

**Collect the 3 Colattaninis**

Spanish support line:

**Colecciona los 3 Colattaninis**

## Format

- Size: Letter, portrait, laminated.
- Counter version: one durable copy near register.
- Optional customer version: unlaminated half-sheet or small card if Colattao wants take-home tracking.
- Finish: matte laminate to reduce glare.
- Corners: rounded if possible.

## Layout

Top:

- Colattao logo or wordmark, using approved logo only.
- Campaign title.
- One-line invite: "Play Penalty Shootout. Win stickers. Collect all 3."

Middle:

Three sticker slots in a row or stacked vertically for narrow counter space:

1. **Churro Chispa**  
   Slot label: Churro Latte  
   Unlock: Beat Street Keeper

2. **Coco Beach Blitzer**  
   Slot label: Coco Beach  
   Unlock: Beat Club Keeper

3. **Cali Croissant Captain**  
   Slot label: California Sandwich  
   Unlock: Beat Pro Keeper

Bottom:

- QR placement.
- Simple instructions.
- Reward note.
- Small staff note: "Show your result screen to staff."

## QR Placement

- Place QR in lower-right or bottom-center.
- Minimum printed QR size: 1.25 in x 1.25 in; 1.5 in preferred.
- QR destination for launch should point to the Penalty Shootout route: `https://finacalleos.com/penalty-shootout`.
- If a Colattao-specific game route is later created, replace the QR only after manual scan testing.
- QR codes must be generated as static assets and phone scan-tested. Do not generate QR codes inside AI image tools.

## Reward Explanation

Use safe MVP copy:

**Collect all 3 and show staff for today's Colattanini reward.**

Spanish:

**Colecciona los 3 y muéstralos al equipo para la recompensa Colattanini del día.**

Do not print a specific discount, free item, or dollar value until the owner approves it.

## Simple Customer Instructions

English:

1. Scan the QR.
2. Pick a keeper.
3. Take five shots.
4. Show staff your win.
5. Collect the matching sticker.

Spanish:

1. Escanea el QR.
2. Elige un portero.
3. Tira cinco penales.
4. Muestra tu victoria al equipo.
5. Colecciona tu sticker.

## Countertop Usability

- Keep the sheet upright or flat beside the register.
- Leave enough whitespace around the QR for easy scanning.
- Do not place the QR near a fold, glare line, or sticker slot edge.
- Use large type that reads from 2-3 feet away.
- Keep instructions short enough that staff can point instead of explaining everything.
- If sample stickers are placed on the laminated sheet, use removable adhesive so the display can be refreshed.

## Approval Checklist

- Menu item labels match verified menu item names.
- QR destination is scan-tested.
- No prices are printed.
- Reward copy is owner-approved.
- Logo source is approved.
- Sheet is readable on a phone photo and at the counter.
