# Colattanini Counter Sign Copy

## Status

Copy options for a small countertop sign. Use only after QR destination and reward wording are approved.

## Recommended Short Copy

English:

**Play Penalty Shootout. Win a Colattanini sticker. Collect all 3.**

Spanish:

**Juega Penalty Shootout. Gana un sticker Colattanini. Colecciona los 3.**

## Friendly Version

English:

**Waiting on your coffee? Take five shots and win a Colattanini sticker.**

Spanish:

**¿Esperando tu café? Tira cinco penales y gana un sticker Colattanini.**

## Menu-Tie Version

English:

**Meet Churro Chispa, Coco Beach Blitzer, and Cali Croissant Captain. Play to collect them all.**

Spanish:

**Conoce a Churro Chispa, Coco Beach Blitzer y Cali Croissant Captain. Juega para coleccionarlos todos.**

## Reward Teaser

English:

**Collect all 3 and show staff for today's Colattanini reward.**

Spanish:

**Colecciona los 3 y muéstralos al equipo para la recompensa Colattanini del día.**

## Tiny QR Label

English:

**Scan to play**

Spanish:

**Escanea para jugar**

## Do Not Use Yet

- "Free drink"
- "Free food"
- "Guaranteed prize"
- Specific discount amounts
- Any price or menu claim not approved by the owner
- Any QR generated inside AI artwork
