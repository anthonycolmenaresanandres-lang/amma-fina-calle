# Project Seed visual cleanup — September 30, 2026

Anthony rejected the cartoon October cup illustrations and the displayed flyer, and explicitly requested execution and merge without tests. The original photo-based product cutouts documented in `FALLING_RUSH_PRODUCT_ART_V1.md` are restored with their original product labels. They are not relabeled as October products. The October menu remains transcribed text; its supplied flyer remains an internal reference asset and is no longer rendered or linked on the page.

The game uses a restrained red roof and warm wall with a geographic Philippines silhouette. No lamps, furniture, foliage, photo collage, or glow overlays remain in the active background. Portrait and landscape WebPs are generated by `APP/web/scripts/project-seed-backdrop.mjs`; the original SVG source is retained beside each output. The prior `october/*-v1.webp` cup and backdrop assets are retired from active use.

The Philippine flag appears at its 2:1 aspect ratio in both menu and game headers. Source: [Wikimedia Commons, Flag of the Philippines](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Philippines.svg), public domain; downloaded unchanged to `brand/philippines-flag.svg`.

The Philippines boundary is the PHL feature from [Natural Earth 1:50m countries](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson), retained at `brand/philippines-boundary.json`. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) place the data in the public domain.

Project Seed's supplied circular logo and the existing noindex concept status remain in use. This record does not claim client sign-off.
