# SOP — Follow-up Cadence and Anti-Spam

## Sales follow-up
- Use the actual last sent message as the clock.
- Do not create a follow-up when a current draft already exists for the same objective unless the new draft is materially different.
- Default sequence when no custom cadence exists: initial outreach → useful follow-up after ~3 business days → final value-adding follow-up after ~7 business days → NURTURE.
- After two unanswered follow-ups, stop active pursuit unless there is a new signal, deadline, referral, event, or explicit reason.
- Never send or draft repetitive 'just checking in' messages.

## Alerts
- Do not repeat the same QA/client/knowledge alert unless severity changed, new evidence appeared, the due date crossed a threshold, or the prior alert remains unresolved past its escalation SLA.
- Every alert should carry a dedupe key based on entity + issue + material state.

## Re-entry
A NURTURE item may return to active only when there is new evidence or a dated planned trigger.
