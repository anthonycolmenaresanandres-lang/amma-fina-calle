# SOP — Severity and Escalation SLA

Use severity to control interruption.

RED:
- active customer/revenue loss, security/access risk, failed critical workflow, binding deadline, or irreversible-action decision.
- surface immediately when verified.

YELLOW:
- meaningful risk/opportunity needing action soon but not an emergency.
- include owner and due/trigger; consolidate into the next normal operating brief when possible.

GREEN:
- healthy / informational / no action.
- do not interrupt Anthony.

Decision aging:
- due within 48h and blocked → YELLOW.
- overdue with material revenue/client impact → RED.
- overdue without material impact → include in decision-debt section, not repeated standalone alerts.

Every escalation must include evidence, owner, next action, and what happens if no action is taken.
