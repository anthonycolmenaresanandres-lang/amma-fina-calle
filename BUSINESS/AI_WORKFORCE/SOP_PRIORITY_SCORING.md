# SOP — Priority Scoring

Use this only to rank work; it is not a substitute for judgment.

Score each candidate action:
- Revenue impact: 0–3
- Urgency/time sensitivity: 0–3
- Confidence/evidence quality: 0–2
- Strategic leverage/reusability: 0–2
- Effort cost: 0–2 penalty

Priority score = (2 × revenue) + urgency + confidence + leverage − effort.

Tie-breakers:
1. existing warm opportunity over cold prospect;
2. client/revenue risk over cosmetic improvement;
3. action that closes a loop over action that starts a new loop;
4. reversible action over irreversible action;
5. verified evidence over intuition.

Workers should normally surface no more than the top 3–5 items.

Hard override:
- safety, legal/compliance, customer trust, and active revenue-loss risks can outrank the numeric score.
