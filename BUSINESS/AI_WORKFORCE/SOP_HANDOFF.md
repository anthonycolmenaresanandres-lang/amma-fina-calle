# SOP — Worker Handoffs

## Required packet
Every inter-agent handoff uses this exact structure:

OWNER:
OBJECTIVE:
VERIFIED EVIDENCE:
CURRENT STATE:
NEXT ACTION:
DUE / TRIGGER:
BLOCKER:
APPROVAL REQUIRED:
SOURCE LINKS / IDS:

## Pipeline stages
RESEARCHED — account/contact verified, no outreach.
DRAFT_READY — targeted outreach prepared.
CONTACTED — send evidence exists.
REPLIED — reply evidence exists.
DISCOVERY — meaningful sales conversation scheduled/held.
DEMO — demonstration scheduled/held.
PROPOSAL — concrete scope/price proposal delivered.
VERBAL_YES — buyer indicated intent; not closed.
WON — agreement/payment evidence exists.
LOST — explicit loss evidence exists.
NURTURE — not active now, with dated reason/follow-up.

Never advance a stage based on intention, scheduled automation, draft creation, or assumption.

## Escalation
Escalate to Executive Ops when:
- two workers disagree on source truth;
- a deadline is within 48 hours and blocked;
- a warm prospect has no owner;
- a client issue risks trust or revenue;
- an action crosses a RED permission boundary.

Executive Ops consolidates. Anthony receives only decisions or high-value actions that need him.
