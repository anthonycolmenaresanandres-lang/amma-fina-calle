# FINA CALLE — OPERATOR RUNBOOK
### How to run a client, start to finish. This is the only page you need.

> You are the **operator**, not the engineer. You don't run the machine — you point it
> at a goal and say yes/no. The AI agents (Claude + Codex) do everything technical.
> When you feel lost: open this page, find which of the **4 jobs** you're on, and hand the rest off.

---

## YOUR 4 JOBS (the only things that need a human)
1. **CLOSE** — get a café to say yes.
2. **COLLECT** — get a few things from them.
3. **APPROVE** — say go / not yet on what the agents build.
4. **BILL** — start the monthly.

Everything between Collect and Approve — the menu page, the game reskin, the launch
video, the deploy — is **not your job.** Hand it to an agent.

---

## THE CLIENT LOOP (do this for every café)

**Step 0 — once, forever:** keep the live Colattao demo on your phone
(`colattao-cafe-rush.vercel.app` + its menu). That's your whole sales pitch.

**Step 1 — CLOSE (you):** Walk in or DM. Show Colattao's menu + game on your phone:
*"We built this for them — yours in days."* Pick a tier, get a yes + the deposit.

**Step 2 — COLLECT (you):** Get these from the owner. This is the ONLY input the system needs:
- Logo (image file)
- Menu (items + prices — photo or text is fine)
- 3–5 photos of the place + signature product
- Owner photos *(optional — only if they want to be on camera)*
- Business name, hours, phone, Instagram, any links
→ Drop it all in a folder and hand it to an agent (see the one line below).

**Step 3 — BUILD (agent — you wait):** The agent makes the menu page, reskins the game,
builds the launch video, and deploys. You do nothing here.

**Step 4 — APPROVE (you):** Agent sends you a preview link. Look at it on your phone.
Looks right? Say "ship it." Something off? Say what, send it back.

**Step 5 — LAUNCH & BILL (you):** Put the QR sticker up, post the launch video,
start the monthly charge. Done. That café is now a reference for the next one.

## THE TIERS (what you're selling) — working numbers, confirm currency (USD vs COP)
- **Menú** — QR digital menu + owner dashboard. **$100 setup + 1st month free.** *(launch after Colattao's results are in)*
- **Juego** — Menú + branded game. Standard **~$1,500–2,000 setup / $250–350 mo.**
- **Marca** — Juego + real website. *Quote.*
- **OS** — Marca + seasonal game/campaign refreshes. *Retainer / quote.*

**Founding Café offer (your closer):** first **2–3 cafés per city** get the premium product
at a locked discount in exchange for a testimonial + being your local reference.
**Colattao = Founding #1, locked at $900 / $150 forever.** Don't quote new clients off that price.
