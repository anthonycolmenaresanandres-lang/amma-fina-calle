# Vital Vibes concept at /vital-vibes

Standalone static GET document based on the reviewed private Vital Vibes concept. It deliberately bypasses the Fina Calle React/customer layouts and analytics, and reads no cookies, identifiers, databases or APIs. Existing customer routes, dependencies and site configuration are unchanged.

The seven pictured product objects open the corresponding screenshot-backed category. Protein Shakes includes Clásico and Especiales; the other headings are Special Drinks, Batidos, Bebidas, Iced Protein, Refrescantes and Bebidas Calientes. Optional labels, native dialogs, visible focus, 44px controls, carousel arrow alternatives and reduced-motion support are retained.

51 legible menu names were transcribed from three screenshots supplied by the user. Source photographs/video chrome and unrelated identities are not included. Original editable SVGs interpret the actual forest/coral identity, dome cups, magenta and purple drinks, chocolate-streak shake, cream/black looping border, gold vase and flowers. The proposed name treatment is not an official logo, and the scene is not a claimed photograph of the premises.

Keep the concise concept/menu-confirmation notice and both HTML/HTTP noindex directives until owner confirmation. Tiny ingredient/allergen text, price/serving mappings, official logo, address, hours and contact remain unverified. The cropped hot-drink list is explicitly incomplete. No prices, recipes, contact data, ordering, accounts or payments are invented or connected.

Checks: `node --import tsx scripts/vital-vibes-selftest.ts`; scoped ESLint for this route, scripts and its two public JS files; normal production build; existing required web CI before merge. Static resources use absolute `/vital-vibes/` paths so both slash forms resolve correctly.
