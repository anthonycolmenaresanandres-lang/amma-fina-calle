# How it works

1. **Scan:** a stable QR opens the restaurant's mobile experience.
2. **Choose:** the guest opens the menu or an approved playable module.
3. **Control:** the authorized owner receives a private portal and completes a mandatory first-login password reset.
4. **Handoff:** Fina Calle verifies the guest QR, owner access, selected game, support path, and any purchased printing before acceptance.

The game shown in sales materials is a **playable engine demo**, not the restaurant's finished game. One existing module may be selected and verified before launch. Custom art or mechanics require a separate written scope.

Not included in the base offer: ordering, payments, checkout, POS, table service, guest/customer accounts, personal-data collection, physical printing, additional owner-account work, or guaranteed results.
