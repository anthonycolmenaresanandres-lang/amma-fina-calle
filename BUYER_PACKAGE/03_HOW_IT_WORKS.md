# How it works

1. **Scan:** a stable QR opens the restaurant's mobile experience.
2. **Choose:** the guest opens the menu or an approved playable module.
3. **Control:** the authorized owner signs in privately to manage menu information.
4. **Support:** Fina Calle hosts the experience and supports the link and agreed updates.

The game shown in sales materials is a **playable engine demo**, not the restaurant's finished game. One existing module may be selected and verified before launch. Custom art or mechanics require a separate written scope.

Not included in the base offer: ordering, payments, checkout, POS, table service, customer accounts, personal-data collection, physical printing, or guaranteed results.
