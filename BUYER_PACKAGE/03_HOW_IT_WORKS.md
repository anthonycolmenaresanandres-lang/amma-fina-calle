# How it works

**One QR. Three things for your customer. One private page for you.** That's the whole idea.

---

## The customer side — one scan

1. **They scan the QR** on the table, counter, or window. No app, no download, no sign-up.
2. **They land on your branded menu** — items, prices, hours, and current promos, clean on their phone.
3. **They can play your branded mini-game** while they wait — a light, fun moment that keeps them
   engaged instead of glancing and leaving.

That's it for the customer. It feels modern, it's fast, and it carries **your** brand — not ours.

## Your side — one private page

- You get a **private, secure sign-in** (just your email) to your own owner page.
- From there you **update your own menu** — items, prices, promos — no phone calls, no waiting.
- Prefer we do it? **Small updates are included** in monthly maintenance. Send us the change.

## Our side — we handle the tech

- **Setup** — we build your menu page and branded experience.
- **Hosting** — it lives on our platform (finacalleos.com), secure (HTTPS).
- **The link + QR flow** — we set up the QR so a scan lands in the right place.
- **Support** — uptime checks, QR link support, and small updates each month.

---

## What it is — and isn't

**It is:** a branded, in-store digital experience behind one QR — menu + mini-game + your owner page.

**It isn't** (today): online ordering, payments, a POS system, a full website rebuild, or customer
data collection. If you ever want more, we scope it separately and in writing — never a surprise.

> **Your brand, your control.** The mini-game shown in demos is a **playable demo of our game
> engine** — your version would be skinned with *your* brand. We don't present a demo as your
> finished game, and we don't build a custom feature until it's scoped with you.
