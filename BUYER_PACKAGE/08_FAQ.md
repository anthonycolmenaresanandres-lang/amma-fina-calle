# Common questions

## “I already have a menu.”

Good. We can link to it or build the agreed mobile menu experience. The goal is one useful QR destination, not unnecessary replacement work.

## “Is this a website?”

It is a focused mobile experience for the menu, an approved playable module, and owner access. It does not have to replace the restaurant's website or Instagram.

## “How much?”

**Starting at $199/month per location.** Setup and custom work are priced separately in writing.

## “What game do I get?”

The demo proves the engine. The written proposal identifies the existing module included for your location. Custom game art or mechanics are separate.

## “Do I get an owner account?”

Yes. The base launch includes one private owner portal and delivery of the initial authorized account. The portal link and one-time credential are delivered through separate channels, and the owner creates a private password at first sign-in. Additional users or training are written separately.

## “Do you print the QR codes?”

Print-ready guest and back-office QR files are included. Physical printing is quoted separately. No print order is placed until the material, size, quantity, price, and physical scan proof are approved.

## “Does it include ordering or payments?”

No. Ordering, payments, checkout, POS, and table service are not included in the base plan.

## “What happens next?”

Ask for the $199/month plan in writing. You can review the scope, terms, exclusions, and next date before approving anything.

## Respuestas rápidas

- **Precio:** desde **$199 al mes por local**.
- **Incluye:** menú móvil, QR estable para clientes, un módulo jugable existente, portal privado, entrega de la primera cuenta autorizada y archivos QR listos para imprimir.
- **Por separado:** instalación fuera del alcance escrito, impresión física, usuarios o capacitación adicional, servicio por mesa, promociones, informes y trabajo personalizado.
- **No incluye:** pedidos, pagos, POS ni resultados garantizados.
