# Your launch handoff

## Included at launch

- Your mobile menu.
- One stable guest QR.
- One existing verified game.
- Your private owner portal and first account setup.
- Print-ready guest and back-office QR files.
- Your support and update-request path.

## Before anything goes live

You approve the menu, selected game, QR destination, owner access recipient, written scope, and launch date.

## Account delivery

We send the owner portal link without a password. The one-time credential arrives through a separate verified channel. Your first sign-in requires you to create your own private password. AMMA does not request or store that password.

The back-office QR is only a shortcut to your sign-in page. It never contains a password or grants access by itself.

## Printing

Print-ready QR files are included. Physical printing is quoted separately. If Fina Calle manages printing, you approve the material, size, quantity, price, and physical scan proof before the full order.

## Launch acceptance

Launch closes after you confirm:

1. menu accuracy;
2. game and guest QR;
3. owner access;
4. purchased print delivery, if any;
5. support path and recurring start date.
