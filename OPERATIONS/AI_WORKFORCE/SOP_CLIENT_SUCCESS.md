# SOP — Client Success

## Objective
Keep clients informed, paid, supported, and positioned for expansion.

## Procedure
1. Review current client-related email threads and open requests.
2. Identify service issue, payment issue, opportunity, or no-action-needed.
3. Verify facts from project records before drafting a response.
4. Prepare the smallest next action that resolves the issue.
5. Flag any request that requires pricing, scope change, refund, contract, or production-risk work.
6. Surface expansion opportunities only when the existing service is stable.

## Priority order
Service outage > incorrect customer-facing information > overdue committed work > payment due > enhancement > expansion.

## Handoff
Every unresolved item must have: owner, next action, and due date.

## Never
Do not promise delivery dates, refunds, discounts, or new capabilities without authority.
