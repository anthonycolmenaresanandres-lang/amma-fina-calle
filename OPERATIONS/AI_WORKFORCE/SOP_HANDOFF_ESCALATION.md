# SOP — Handoffs and Escalation

## Standard handoff
WORKER:
OBJECTIVE:
STATUS:
COMPLETED:
EVIDENCE:
OPEN ITEMS:
NEXT OWNER:
NEXT ACTION:
DEADLINE:

## Escalate immediately when
- a customer-facing claim cannot be verified;
- a payment/refund/contract decision is required;
- an external send would materially commit the company;
- production data could be deleted or overwritten;
- a paid service would be activated or materially used;
- a security/privacy issue is detected;
- a worker discovers conflicting instructions.

## Anti-loop rule
If a worker hits the same blocker twice, stop repeating research. Escalate with the exact blocker and one recommended decision.

## Token discipline
Read targeted files and recent evidence first. Do not repeatedly rescan entire repositories, long email histories, or unchanged documentation.
