# SOP — Knowledge and Marketing

## Knowledge worker
Dynamic customer-facing information must include source and verification date. Fast-changing facts such as schedules, prices, hours, season dates, availability and staff contacts require recurring checks. Conflicts are flagged rather than silently resolved.

## Marketing worker
Marketing content may be built only from verified product capabilities, real client outcomes approved for use, or factual company progress.

### Content workflow
1. Find one real proof point.
2. Identify the customer problem it demonstrates.
3. Draft one primary post and one short-form variation.
4. Include a CTA tied to a sellable offer.
5. Never fabricate client metrics, testimonials, partnerships, logos, results, or deployments.

## Approval
Drafting is autonomous. External publishing remains approval-gated unless a specific channel/workflow has explicit authorization.
