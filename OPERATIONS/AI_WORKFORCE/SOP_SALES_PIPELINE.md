# SOP — Sales Pipeline

## Objective
Convert existing Fina Calle capabilities into conversations, demos, proposals, and paid pilots.

## Daily procedure
1. Review replies and due follow-ups before cold prospecting.
2. Select no more than five highest-value revenue actions.
3. Verify prospect name, business, role, and relevance.
4. Match one existing offer to one concrete business problem.
5. Prepare a short personalized outreach draft.
6. Record status: researched / drafted / sent / replied / demo / proposal / won / lost.
7. Escalate to Anthony before external sending unless that workflow has send authorization.

## Qualified offer set
- Restaurant digital menu + interactive customer experience.
- AI Front Desk / information and lead-capture assistant.
- Sports GameDay Content / verified result-to-social workflow.
- Custom automation only when it reuses proven components.

## Qualification gate
Do not pitch a feature marked planned, experimental, stale, or unverified as production-ready.

## Demo standard
Show working behavior first; architecture second; roadmap last.

## KPIs
Contacts, replies, demos booked, proposals, setup revenue, MRR.
