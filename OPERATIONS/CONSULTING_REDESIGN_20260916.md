# Fina Calle consulting-first redesign

Authority: Anthony confirmed family-owned consulting, hands-on digital delivery, original logo/black-gold/motion preservation, truthful proof, visible mobile explanations, consultation/support separation and secondary restaurant packages starting at $199/month/location. He subsequently authorized execution, merge and live release.

## Design decision

Keep the existing crest-to-Colattao-photo motion as the signature. Retain Bodoni Moda display and Geist body/utility. Use Ink #07090b, Graphite #0d1115, Gold #c8aa72, Porcelain #f0ece4 and readable muted copy #bdc1c4. Retain existing sapphire in the identity/motion. Open sections and text-led project entries preserve the brand without inventing people or business history.

Chosen flow: [consulting proposition + original crest] -> [live client menu + labeled demos] -> [how we help] -> [written-scope delivery process] -> [secondary restaurant offer] -> [consultation / separate support]. A package-first alternative was rejected because it would obscure the approved consulting positioning. Essential text stays visible on phones.

## Primary-source research

- Work & Co: https://www.work.co/ and https://www.work.co/process/ — plain proposition, named work, delivery process.
- Thoughtworks: https://www.thoughtworks.com/what-we-do — service directions connected with client evidence.
- Reason One: https://reasononeinc.com/digital-roadmapping-services/ — buyer situation, deliverables and scope/price clarity.

These are design observations, not evidence of conversion lift for Fina Calle.

## Evidence and copy boundaries

- Known: Anthony supplied family-owned positioning. Current public Colattao menu renders categories/items/prices at https://colattao-cafe-rush.vercel.app/menu. Penalty Shootout and Las Palmas review routes return expected public content. Las Palmas explicitly remains a demo pending client approval.
- Inferred design priority: clarity and buyer trust, from Anthony's instruction to distinguish live work, demos and scope. No psychological priority is assigned to an individual prospect.
- Missing: verified conversion data, testimonials, expanded family biography, client-specific owner activation and business-results attribution. None is invented or displayed.
- Recognition -> outcome -> proof -> bounded offer -> action: business problem -> practical direction and digital delivery -> current public work -> written scope -> Start a consultation. No free consultation, fixed response SLA or unapproved pilot terms are promised.
- Restaurant price/terms: SALES_DEMO_PACKAGE/CORE_OFFER_199.md. Starting at $199/month per location; setup/timing/update limits/recurring terms defined before approval. Consulting/custom work and physical printing separately scoped.
- Colattao's separate public menu is not represented as connected to live owner self-editing. Public demo availability is not proof of restaurant approval or activated billing/access.

## Optional future measurement

Hypothesis: clearer consulting language improves the proportion of inquiries with a concrete business need. H-CONSULTING-01 / P-PUBLIC-WORK-01 / C-CONSULTATION-01. Primary metric: qualified inquiries divided by recorded consultation exposures. Guardrail: support requests misrouted as new consultations and reports of pricing/demo confusion. Stop on a broken proof link or any misunderstanding that a demo is an approved client product or that custom consulting is included in $199. No experiment tracking, customer contact or winning result is claimed in this release.
