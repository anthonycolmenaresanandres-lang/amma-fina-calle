# Colattanini Staff Playbook

## Purpose

Give staff a simple, low-friction way to run the Colattanini collectible campaign at the counter without new software, logins, customer accounts, POS changes, or customer-data collection.

## What Staff Says

Short English script:

> Want to play for a Colattanini sticker while you wait? Scan here, pick a keeper, take five shots, and show us your win.

Short Spanish script:

> ¿Quieres jugar por un sticker Colattanini mientras esperas? Escanea aquí, elige un portero, tira cinco penales y muéstranos tu victoria.

Menu tie:

> Each sticker is inspired by a real Colattao menu item: Churro Latte, Coco Beach, and California Sandwich.

## How To Give Stickers

1. Customer scans the QR and plays Penalty Shootout.
2. Customer shows the result screen.
3. Staff checks which keeper level was played and whether the result qualifies.
4. Staff gives the matching sticker from behind the counter.
5. If the customer already has that sticker, invite them to try the next keeper level.
6. If a sticker is out of stock, offer another available Colattanini or ask the customer to come back.

## How To Verify A Win

Fast MVP verification:

- Ask the customer to show the final game screen.
- Confirm the keeper level shown or selected.
- Confirm the score/result meets the owner-approved threshold.
- Do not ask for the customer's name, email, phone number, social handle, or screenshot.

Recommended threshold for launch:

- A clear win or staff-approved score on the selected keeper level.
- If the final UI does not clearly say "win" yet, staff can use the simplest rule: **3 or more goals out of 5 qualifies**. This threshold should be confirmed before printing.

## Full Collection Reward

When a customer shows all three Colattanini stickers:

1. Congratulate them.
2. Confirm all three characters are present.
3. Apply the current owner-approved reward.
4. If no reward has been approved yet, use this line:

> You completed the set. The reward is being finalized, so check with us next visit.

Do not invent a discount or free item at the counter unless Anthony/Colattao has approved it.

## Rules

- One sticker per verified qualifying result.
- No purchase requirement in the fastest MVP unless the owner approves one.
- No personal-data tracking.
- No POS changes.
- No payment or loyalty integration.
- Stickers are while supplies last.
- Staff may use kindness/honor-rule discretion for kids and families during the first launch week.
- If the cafe is busy, staff can say: "Play while you wait, then show us when you're done."

## Shift Checklist

Before shift:

- Put the laminated collector sheet on the counter.
- Confirm QR scans.
- Place sticker stacks behind the counter.
- Check which character is lowest.

During shift:

- Invite customers waiting on drinks/food.
- Hand stickers only after result-screen verification.
- Keep the line moving; do not over-explain.

End of shift:

- Count remaining stickers.
- Note any customer confusion.
- Note whether one keeper level feels too hard/easy for the reward.

## Escalation

Tell Anthony if:

- QR does not scan.
- Game route is down.
- Customers cannot tell whether they won.
- One sticker runs out.
- Customers ask about the full-collection reward.
- Staff wants to change the threshold or sticker rule.
