# The Fina Calle owner handbook

Version 1 | September 14, 2026

## A little less admin. More time for your guests.

Your owner portal brings menu tools, account information, payments and a direct line to Fina Calle into one private workspace. This handbook explains the available tools, not a promise that every tool is activated for every business. Follow the status and setup notices shown in your own account.

## Start with the right door

1. Scan the owner QR supplied by Fina Calle or open your exact owner link. Check the business name and the **finacalleos.com** address before signing in.
2. Enter the email assigned to your business and your private password. If prompted, choose a new private password before continuing.
3. Check that the correct business and account information appear. Stop and contact Fina Calle if the name, access or information is wrong.

Keep the owner QR in a staff-only area. It is different from the guest menu QR. Scanning it does not grant access or enroll you in payments. Never share a password or send it to Fina Calle. Sign out on shared devices.

## Find the task you need

- **Edit menu:** update one existing item and one field at a time when the guest menu is connected.
- **Payments:** view account details and use the available Stripe billing controls. Review terms before any payment or enrollment.
- **Contact:** ask Fina Calle for help, request a larger change or send supporting files.
- **History:** review recent recorded changes. This is not a full activity report or an undo button.
- **Owner guide:** open the current online instructions at finacalleos.com/owner/guide.

## If you cannot sign in

Check the exact owner link and assigned email. Contact **Ammaventuresvb@gmail.com** with your business name and the error you see. Do not send your password, bank credentials or full card number. Account access must be confirmed; a public sign-in page is not proof of an activated account.

# Small menu changes, clearly reviewed

## Change one item, then the next

When direct editing is connected, use it for an existing item's base price, name, description or availability. Existing size prices are edited separately. For one or two items, finish the first change and verify it before starting the second; this is not a bulk editor.

1. Open **Edit menu**. Choose the item and the field you want to change.
2. Enter the new value. Select **Review change** and compare Current with After saving.
3. Select **Save menu change**. Open the connected guest menu and refresh to confirm what guests see.

Your permanent guest QR stays the same when the connected menu is updated. A saved record is not proof that a separate website or printed price list changed. Follow any connection notice in your portal.

## Useful details

- Enter prices with up to two decimal places. A zero price means **Ask staff**, not a free item.
- Use Availability to mark an item sold out; restore Available when it returns. Check the guest view after each save.
- New items, categories, photos and larger changes go through **Contact**.
- If another person changed the same field, reload and review the current value. Do not repeatedly submit an old edit.
- **History** shows recent recorded changes, not every action. To correct a value, make a new reviewed edit or contact the team.

## Colattao: request changes to the separate guest site

Colattao's guest menu runs on a separate website. Direct owner edits are **not connected to that site**. Choose **Request a menu update** or **Contact** and give the exact item, current wording and requested change. A sent request is not confirmation that the update is live. Ask for confirmation and check the guest site after completion.

## Send a clear request

Open **Contact Fina Calle**. Include what should change, where it appears, the exact replacement and your requested deadline. Select **Review request**, check the summary, then **Send request** when offered. Keep the request reference.

Messages allow up to **4,000 characters** with up to **five JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF files**, each no larger than **4 MB**. Follow any upload-retry message; do not assume every attachment arrived. A proposed direct change requires a separate **Confirm change**. Team-reviewed requests are not instant changes.

# Your account and payments

## Check your account information

Open **Payments** to review the business, contact and billing information available for your account. Missing details or setup-pending notices need Fina Calle's attention. Use **Contact** to request corrections; the account summary is not a general-purpose profile editor.

## View invoices and pay when needed

When your billing account is connected, select **Invoices & payment methods**. Stripe opens the available invoice and payment-management tools. Review the business and amount before confirming any payment. View or download invoices and receipts when available. Available options depend on the account and provider settings.

Only enter payment details on the expected secure Stripe page. Fina Calle's owner QR is not a payment QR. A saved payment method does not, by itself, start a new recurring subscription.

## Automatic payments are your choice

**Set up automatic payments** is a separate, explicit enrollment. It is available only after your account has an approved recurring amount, currency, billing interval and first-charge date at least **48 hours away**, matching the configured plan. If terms are missing, mismatched or out of date, contact Fina Calle instead of enrolling.

1. Review the amount, billing frequency and first-charge date in your portal.
2. Open **Set up automatic payments** only if you want recurring billing. Review the final Stripe terms and confirmation before consenting.
3. After returning, verify the confirmed billing status and any invoice. A return message or URL is not proof that enrollment or payment succeeded.

Already enrolled? Use **Manage automatic payments** or the invoice-management control for the existing account. Do not create another enrollment to fix a payment problem. An active subscription does not necessarily mean every invoice has been paid. Manage available payment-method or subscription settings in Stripe; contact Fina Calle if the needed control is unavailable.

## Zelle, only when shown and configured

Use only the verified recipient displayed inside your signed-in portal. Pay from your bank app, then select **Report payment sent** with the amount and an optional note. **Reported** means awaiting Fina Calle's reconciliation, not paid or verified. Do not send a second payment merely because verification is pending.

# Keep the portal close. Keep access private.

## Add it to your home screen

- **iPhone or iPad:** open the exact owner link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open the owner link in Chrome and look for Install app or Add to Home screen.
- **Desktop:** Chrome or Edge may offer an install icon or Install app command.

Browser options vary. Check that the shortcut returns to your business. Internet and authorized sign-in are still required. Installation does not activate access or billing, or enable offline edits or payments.

## When something does not look right

- **Wrong business or missing access:** stop and contact Fina Calle.
- **Guest menu unchanged:** check the connection notice and refresh the correct guest link. Use Contact for Colattao's separate site. A separate ordering provider does not automatically update.
- **Unclear payment status:** check Stripe's invoice and subscription status. Contact Fina Calle before paying or enrolling again.
- **Missing attachment or incorrect account detail:** retry the attachment using the same reference, or request the correction through Contact.

## What this portal does not promise

This is not a POS replacement. It does not establish table-service or ordering integration, instant team replies, bulk publishing, offline payments or a live guest-feedback inbox. Guest feedback and owner support are separate channels. Availability depends on your approved setup.

## Stay in touch

Use **Contact** for a tracked request. Cannot sign in? Email **Ammaventuresvb@gmail.com** with your business name and issue, never a password. Current guide: **https://finacalleos.com/owner/guide**.
